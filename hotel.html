About HOTEL

Nestled among the tree-lined avenues of Dehradun, HOTEL has been a cherished landmark for travellers seeking an authentic blend of heritage grandeur and modern comfort. Our property reflects the timeless spirit of the Doon Valley — gracious, warm, and deeply rooted in tradition.

Whether you are visiting for business, a family celebration, or a quiet weekend retreat, every stay at HOTEL is crafted around one simple promise: to make you feel at home, with an elegance you will remember long after you leave.

The Grand Kitchen

A culinary journey through the flavours of Uttarakhand and beyond

The Grand Kitchen is our in-house restaurant where tradition meets taste. Our chefs draw inspiration from the rich culinary heritage of the Garhwal and Kumaon hills, serving dishes that celebrate local ingredients alongside beloved classics from across India and the world.

Whether you are starting your morning with a hearty Pahadi breakfast, enjoying a leisurely lunch with family, or savouring a candlelit dinner, every meal at The Grand Kitchen is prepared with care and served with warmth.

🌟 Signature Dishes

Garhwali Dal Tadka

Slow-cooked black lentils tempered with local spices, served with steamed rice and ghee

Tandoori Jhinga Platter

Succulent prawns marinated in yogurt and charcoal-grilled to smoky perfection

Signature Butter Chicken

Our house-special creamy tomato curry with tender chicken, finished with a swirl of cream

🏔️ Local Favourites

Kafuli with Mandua Roti

A beloved Garhwali spinach preparation served with traditional finger-millet flatbread

Chainsoo & Bhatt Ki Churkani

A hearty Kumaoni lentil dish with black soybeans, flavoured with local herbs

Bal Mithai

Traditional Kumaoni chocolate fudge coated in sugar balls — the perfect sweet ending
